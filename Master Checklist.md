# Submission
- [ ] Report `OSCP-OS-XXXXX-Exam-Report.pdf`*
- [ ] **Passwordless** `OSCP-OS-XXXXX-Exam-Report.7z`* archive containing report
	- `sudo 7z a OSCP-OS-XXXXX-Exam-Report.7z OSCP-OS-XXXXX-Exam-Report.pdf`
- [ ] Archive not bigger than 200MB
- [ ] Archive uploaded to [https://upload.offsec.com](https://upload.offsec.com)
- [ ] **Verify md5 checksums**

\* _(where `OS-XXXXX` is your OSID)_
